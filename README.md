# IT Products Sales Dashboard

> A comprehensive sales performance and analytics workbook designed to track IT product sales, customer regions, store performance, and revenue insights. Marked as a practice project for data analysis, spreadsheet modeling, and dashboard reporting.

![Dashboard](Dashboard%20Images/it-products-sales-dashboard.png)

## Questions answered
- How many total orders and sales revenue do we have? (24 orders; 460,650 total sales)

- Sales by product category and top-selling items (Laptops, Mobile phones, Accessories)

- Regional sales performance across North, South, East, and West regions

- Monthly sales trend and store-wise distribution

- Total cost, profit, and profit margin analysis


## How it works
- Source data is structured in multiple Excel tables (Customers, Products, Stores, Sales); all analysis uses PivotTables.

- Summary cards and metrics stay dynamically linked to the pivot structures.

- Slicers and filters allow dynamic exploration of sales data by region and product category.

## Tools
Microsoft Excel: PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, data modeling, dashboard design.
